How it works

From first call to real progress

Getting started takes three simple steps. Here’s exactly what to expect.

01. Free assessment

We get to know your child, find their strengths and gaps, and talk through your goals as a family.

  • No cost, no commitment
  • About 30–45 minutes, in person or online
  • You’ll leave with a clear picture of where your child stands

02. A plan built for your child

Your tutor creates a personalized learning plan around how your child learns and what they’re working on in school.

  • Clear goals and milestones
  • Matched with a tutor who fits your child’s needs and schedule
  • Plan covers academics, organization, and confidence

03. Sessions and progress

Regular one-on-one sessions, with updates so you always know how things are going.

  • Usually once or twice a week
  • In your home, another location, or online
  • Plans adjust as your child grows
