Tutoring

Support for every subject, every stage

  • We find the gaps underneath what your child is learning in class and close them first, so each new topic has something solid to stand on. Every step gets worked out together until it makes sense on their own.

    • Number sense
    • Fractions & decimals
    • Pre-algebra
    • Algebra I & II
    • Geometry
    • Pre-calculus

  • Phonics and fluency for early readers, comprehension and vocabulary as the books get harder. We work with students with dyslexia and other learning differences, at their own pace.

    • Phonics
    • Fluency
    • Comprehension
    • Vocabulary
    • Close reading

  • We break writing into steps students can follow: brainstorm, outline, draft, revise. Kids learn to get their ideas onto the page and organize them clearly, from a first paragraph to a full research paper.

    • Sentence structure
    • Paragraphs
    • Essays
    • Research papers
    • Grammar & editing

  • Focused prep for the SOLs, SAT, and ACT. We review the content, practice under real timing, and teach the strategies that keep nerves from getting in the way on test day.

    • SOLs
    • SAT
    • ACT
    • Finals
    • Practice tests
    • Test anxiety

  • Schools expect kids to manage time and materials but rarely teach how. We build the habits that make schoolwork manageable: tracking assignments, breaking big projects into steps, and getting started without a fight.

    • Organization
    • Time management
    • Planning
    • Study skills
    • Focus

  • Your child brings what’s due and we work through it together, explaining as we go so they understand it, not just finish it. A steady hand for busy weeks and the subjects that are hard to help with at home.

    • All subjects
    • Daily assignments
    • Projects
    • Quiz & test review
