Common struggles
at this age

Middle and high school bring more classes, more teachers, and more homework. Gaps that were small before start to show.

  • Falling behind in math

    Each unit builds on the last, so one missed step turns into a string of low grades.

  • Missing assignments

    Homework gets lost, deadlines slip, and big projects start the night before they’re due.

  • Freezing on big tests

    They know the material, but SOLs, the SAT, and finals bring on nerves when it counts.
