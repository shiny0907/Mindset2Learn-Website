Common struggles
in college

College moves faster and checks in less. Without someone keeping them on track, students can fall behind before anyone notices.

  • Tough intro courses

    Calculus, statistics, and science classes move fast, and office hours aren’t always enough.

  • No one checking in

    Big deadlines are weeks apart, and there’s no one reminding them to get started.

  • Writing at a new level

    Research papers and lab reports expect much more than high school essays did.
